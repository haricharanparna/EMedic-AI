# Prevention & Wellness

## Vaccines for adults
- **Flu:** every year for everyone 6 months and older, ideally by the end of October.
- **COVID-19:** follow current CDC guidance. Ask your provider which updated vaccine is right for you.
- **Tdap/Td** (tetanus, diphtheria, whooping cough): a booster every **10 years**, and Tdap during each pregnancy.
- **Shingles (Shingrix):** 2 doses for adults **50 and older**.
- **Pneumococcal (pneumonia):** adults **50 and older**, and younger adults with certain conditions.
- **RSV:** adults **75 and older**, and some younger adults at higher risk.
Most pharmacies and primary care offices give vaccines, often with no appointment. EMedic AI can help you find a provider near you.

## Recommended screenings by age
- **Blood pressure:** every year from age 40, or every 3–5 years for ages 18–39 with normal readings
- **Cervical cancer:** Pap test every 3 years from **21–29**. From **30–65**, a Pap every 3 years or an HPV test every 5 years
- **Breast cancer:** mammogram every 2 years from **40–74**
- **Colorectal cancer:** starting at **45** through 75 (colonoscopy every 10 years or a stool test more often)
- **Lung cancer:** yearly low-dose CT for ages **50–80** with a heavy smoking history
- **Diabetes:** ages **35–70** who are overweight
Your provider may recommend different timing based on your family history.

## Healthy habits that matter most
- **Move:** at least **150 minutes** of moderate activity a week (like brisk walking) plus muscle-strengthening on 2 days.
- **Sleep:** adults need **7 or more hours** a night.
- **Eat:** fill half your plate with vegetables and fruit, and choose whole grains and lean proteins.
- **Limit:** alcohol, sugary drinks and highly processed foods.
- **Don't smoke or vape.** EMedic's free quit program offers coaching and medicine.

## Annual physicals
A yearly checkup (annual physical) is usually **covered at no cost** by most insurance plans as preventive care. Your provider reviews your health history, checks your vitals, updates vaccines and orders screenings you're due for. Bring a list of your medicines and any questions. If you bring up a new problem, that part of the visit may be billed separately.
