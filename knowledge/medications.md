# Medications

## Acetaminophen (Tylenol) safety
Acetaminophen relieves pain and fever. Always follow the label. For regular over-the-counter use, most adults shouldn't take more than **3,000 mg in 24 hours** unless a provider tells you otherwise. Take less if you drink alcohol regularly or have liver disease. Acetaminophen is hidden in many cold, flu and sleep products, so **check labels so you don't double up**. Too much can cause serious liver damage.

## Ibuprofen and other NSAIDs
Ibuprofen (Advil, Motrin) and naproxen (Aleve) reduce pain, fever and swelling. For ibuprofen, the usual adult over-the-counter dose is **200–400 mg every 4–6 hours**, no more than **1,200 mg in 24 hours** unless a provider directs otherwise. Take it with food. Ask a provider first if you have stomach ulcers or bleeding, kidney disease, heart disease or high blood pressure, take blood thinners, or are pregnant (especially after 20 weeks).

## Antibiotics: when they help
Antibiotics treat infections caused by **bacteria**, like strep throat and most UTIs. They **don't work on viruses** like colds, flu, COVID-19 or most cases of bronchitis. Taking them when they aren't needed can cause side effects and makes future infections harder to treat. If you're prescribed antibiotics, take them exactly as directed and don't share or save leftovers.

## Missed doses and taking medicine safely
If you miss a dose, the general rule is to take it when you remember, **unless it's almost time for your next dose**. Then skip it. **Never double up.** Some medicines have different rules, so check the label or ask a pharmacist. Keep an up-to-date list of all your medicines and supplements, and bring it to every visit.

## Prescription refills
Request refills **3–5 business days before you run out**. If your prescription has refills left, it's usually ready at EMedic Pharmacy within **24 hours**. If there are no refills left, your provider must approve it, which takes up to **2 business days**. Some medicines need a visit before they can be renewed. **Controlled medications** (like opioids, ADHD stimulants and benzodiazepines) can't be refilled through chat or the portal. Contact your provider's office.
