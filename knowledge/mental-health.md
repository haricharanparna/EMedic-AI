# Mental Health

## Signs of anxiety
Anxiety can feel like constant worry, restlessness, trouble concentrating, irritability, muscle tension, trouble sleeping, or a racing heart. Some people have panic attacks: sudden intense fear with chest tightness, shortness of breath and dizziness. Anxiety is very treatable with therapy (especially CBT), medicine, or both. Slow breathing, regular exercise and less caffeine can help in the meantime.

## Signs of depression
Depression is more than feeling sad. Signs that last **2 weeks or longer** include low mood, losing interest in things you used to enjoy, changes in sleep or appetite, low energy, trouble concentrating, and feeling worthless or hopeless. Depression is common and treatable. Talk to your provider or EMedic Behavioral Health. If you're having thoughts of suicide, **call or text 988** right away.

## Sleep problems
For better sleep: keep the same wake-up time every day, avoid caffeine after noon, keep your bedroom cool and dark, put screens away 30–60 minutes before bed, and get up if you can't fall asleep within about 20 minutes. If insomnia lasts more than a few weeks, talk to your provider. **CBT for insomnia** works better long term than sleeping pills.
