# EMedic Services

## Hours and how EMedic works
EMedic is a **virtual care** service: every visit is **by video**, so you can see an EMedic nurse from anywhere in the U.S.
- **Video visits:** **7 AM–11 PM every day**
- **24/7 Nurse Line:** (555) 010-6877
- **Need to be seen in person?** Ask EMedic AI to **find a provider near you**. It searches real licensed clinicians by ZIP code or city in the public U.S. NPI Registry. For urgent problems, go to a local urgent care center, and for emergencies call **911**.

## Booking, changing or canceling appointments
Book through the MyEMedic portal or app, here in chat, or by calling **(555) 010-2273**. All visits are by video. Same-day sick visits open up every morning at 7 AM. Please cancel or reschedule at least **24 hours ahead**. After a second missed appointment without notice, there's a **$40 no-show fee**.

## Video visits (telehealth)
Video visits are available **7 AM–11 PM every day** with an EMedic provider. They're covered by most insurance plans, or **$49 self-pay**. They work well for colds and flu, pink eye, rashes, UTIs, allergies, medication refills and mental health. They're **not for emergencies** like chest pain or trouble breathing. When you book, you get a **personal video link** in the chat (and in your confirmation email if you add one). It opens in your browser on any phone or computer with a camera, so there's **no app to install**. Join about 5 minutes early.

## MyEMedic patient portal
In the MyEMedic portal you can message your care team, see test results, request refills, book visits, pay bills and download your records. Sign up with the activation code on your visit summary, or verify your identity online. Messages to your care team are answered within **2 business days**. Don't use messages for urgent problems.
<!-- keywords: portal mychart myemedic login sign up account message app online -->

## Test results
Most lab results appear in MyEMedic within **1–3 days**. Imaging results take **2–5 days**. By law, some results may be released to you **before your provider has reviewed them**. Your care team will contact you about anything that needs follow-up. If you haven't heard back within a week, send a portal message.

## Insurance accepted
EMedic accepts most major plans, including Aetna, Blue Cross Blue Shield, Cigna, UnitedHealthcare, Medicare and state Medicaid plans. Plans and networks change, so confirm with your insurance company that EMedic is **in-network** for you before your visit. Have your insurance card and a photo ID ready for every visit.

## Bills and payment plans
You can view and pay bills in MyEMedic. For balances over $100, EMedic offers **interest-free payment plans for up to 12 months**. Questions about a bill? Call Patient Billing at **(555) 010-2455**, Monday–Friday 8 AM–6 PM.

## Financial assistance
If you're uninsured or have trouble paying, you may qualify for **free or discounted care**. Households earning up to **300% of the federal poverty level** can apply. Apply in MyEMedic, or book a call with a financial counselor. You can apply before or after you get care.
<!-- keywords: financial assistance charity care afford uninsured no insurance discount help paying -->

## Medical records
Your visit notes, test results and immunizations are free to view and download in MyEMedic. To request your **full medical record** or send records to another provider, submit a Records Request form in the portal. Requests are usually completed within **15 days**, and electronic copies are free.
