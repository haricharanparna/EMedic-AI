What is EMedic AI?

EMedic AI is your 24/7 virtual care assistant. It's like texting a knowledgeable front desk and nurse line at the same time. Ask any health question and get a clear answer from EMedic's health library, with the source shown, so you know it isn't made up.

It also gets things done for you. It can check your symptoms and tell you where to go, refill a prescription, book or change an appointment, and connect you with a real nurse. The nurse sees a summary, so you never have to repeat yourself.

What you can do

  • Ask any health questionFevers, medicines, blood pressure, vaccines, sleep, anxiety, and more.
  • Check your symptomsFind out if you need the ER, urgent care, a video visit, or rest at home.
  • Refill a prescriptionSend a refill request and track it with your RX number.
  • Book or change a video visitSee an EMedic nurse from anywhere in the U.S. Reschedule or cancel with your APT number.
  • Reach a nurse 24/7Live chat, phone, email, or request a callback.
  • Find a provider anywhere in the U.S.Real licensed doctors, dentists and therapists near any ZIP code or city.
  • Get live updatesWhen a nurse approves your refill, you'll see it right in the chat.

How to use it

  1. Tell us who it's for: yourself, your child, or someone you care for.
  2. Type your question the way you'd text a friend, or tap a suggestion.
  3. Tap the buttons when EMedic AI walks you through a task. Type cancel anytime to stop.
  4. Want a person? Type "talk to a nurse" whenever you like.
  5. Keep your numbers. Say "status of RX-123456" or "reschedule APT-123456" later.

Safety first

Emergency? Call 911. Chest pain, trouble breathing, stroke signs, or severe bleeding need help right now, not a chat. In crisis? Call or text 988 for free, 24/7 support.

EMedic AI gives general health information, not a diagnosis or medical advice. It will never prescribe medicine or calculate a dose for a specific person. This is a demo with a fictional health system, so please don't enter real personal or health information.
